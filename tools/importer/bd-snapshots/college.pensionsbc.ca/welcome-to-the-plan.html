Welcome to the plan

Discover why joining BC’s College Pension Plan means working toward a reliable retirement income and how you can make the most of plan membership.

Get started now with My Account

My Account is your secure gateway to your pension information and useful tools. It’s also where you can go to contact the plan if you have questions about your pension.

Getting started with My Account is as easy as 1, 2, 3:

  1. Register for My Account
    • Once you’ve registered, you'll receive email notifications when important pension information is available
  2. Confirm your personal information using My Account
    • Check and update your personal and contact information on your My Account profile page
  3. Name your primary and alternate beneficiaries
    • Protect the financial security of your loved ones or support an organization important to you by naming your beneficiaries
