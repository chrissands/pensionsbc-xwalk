Join our participant list - College

Join our participant list

Help us improve your pension experience by joining our participant list

From time to time, we may invite you to take part in research activities such as surveys, interviews, focus groups, and testing online tools. Your participation will help us improve our pension websites, online tools, and communications.

Sign up for the participant list

Takes less than three minutes to sign up

What happens after you sign up

1

Tell us about yourself

Complete a short survey about yourself. Your answers help us identify research opportunities that may be a good match for you.

2

Participate in member research

When you join our participant list, you will receive invitations to participate in research activities that may include:

  • Surveys
  • Focus group discussions
  • Interviews
  • Testing one of our online tools (for example, the pension estimator or an online form)

Each invitation will include details about the research topic and tasks.

We will be mindful of how often we contact you and will only send occasional invitations to participate.

3

Taking part is always your choice

Joining our participant list does not mean you have to participate in a study. When you receive an invitation, we encourage you to ask questions as part of your decision-making process to participate or not participate.

You can accept or decline any invitation. You can also leave the participant list at any time by emailing memberexperience@pensionsbc.ca.

How to contact us

Contact us at memberexperience@pensionsbc.ca if you:

  • Have questions about joining or leaving the participant list
  • Have questions about a research activity you have been invited to participate in
  • Need to update your contact information
  • Have general questions about member research and user experience

We will reply within three business days.

For all other pension-related inquiries, please contact your plan through our Contact Us page.

How we collect and use your information

Your personal information is being collected by BC Pension Corporation for pension plan administrative services, including the evaluation of such services. This collection is authorized under sections 26(c) and 26(e) of the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act. If you have any questions about this collection, contact BC Pension Corporation's Privacy Team at penc.privacy@pensionsbc.ca or by phone at 778-698-6483.
