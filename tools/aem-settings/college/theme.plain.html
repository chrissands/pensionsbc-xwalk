Site theme

Colours and button shape for the College Pension Plan site. Change a field in the Site theme block, publish this page, and every College page follows on its next load. Leave a field empty to keep the site's own style.

Colours that would be hard to read aren't used: buttons and links need 4.5:1 contrast, the accent colour 3:1. Each colour below shows its ratio.
